## Sam on HyperBEAM

### Quick read:
Good progress on making AO node storage paths less fragile. Sam merged a fix so HyperBEAM keeps the node message `Opts` when reading from, or caching through, remote stores.

The practical effect is simple: fewer weird cases where a read or cache call behaves one way locally, then loses needed settings when it goes through a gateway or another node-backed store. For builders and operators, that means fewer edge-case failures in the paths AO apps depend on to fetch and store messages.

This fits the recent pattern in Sam’s HyperBEAM work: small fixes in message upload, encoding, read-back, and storage behavior that make the system more predictable in real use.

### Why it matters:
Remote storage and gateway paths are part of how AO apps share and retrieve data across nodes. Keeping options intact makes those paths easier to trust, easier to test, and less likely to fail only in distributed setups.

### What stands out:
The work is still focused on practical reliability: not new surface area, but fewer places where message handling can silently change as it moves through the node.
