## Sam on HyperBEAM

Quick read:  
Good progress on faster build-test loops for HyperBEAM feature work. Sam added Forge options to filter which device tests run, plus timeouts so a bad test is less likely to hang the whole flow.

Why it matters:  
AO needs many small pieces of functionality to be easy to build, share, install, and check. Better Forge test controls help turn those pieces into safer, more repeatable work instead of one-off repo knowledge.
